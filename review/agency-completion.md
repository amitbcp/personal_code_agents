## Transferable ideas

- **Literature and knowledge maps:** anthropological emic-before-etic interpretation, historians' dated context and separation of fact/consensus/debate/speculation, and narrative promise-to-evidence maps. Preserve competing interpretations and distinguish a source's stated claim from the reviewer's inference.
- **Paper learning:** separate learning objectives, practice and demonstrated understanding from satisfaction; use essential/deeper/specialist reading layers and a concrete attempted explanation followed by focused practice. These are procedures, not reasons for another standing persona.
- **Critical review:** independent estimates before group discussion, disconfirming evidence, pre-mortems and observation before psychological or causal interpretation. Avoid mandatory findings, fixed confidence scores and forced framework citations.
- **Experiments and collection:** explicit query matrices, collection windows, language/exclusion rules, source URLs and independence versus amplification; cohort-aligned denominators, missingness, lag and uncertainty. Publication or expert signoff alone does not validate a claim.
- **Vision and spatial work:** controlled prompt dimensions; cultural, temporal and physical-consistency slices; semantic and coordinate-datum preservation; resolution versus accuracy and independent checkpoints.
- **Pipelines and migrations:** immutable inputs, per-transform counts, expected versus observed states, queue/wait/rework analysis, explicit dependency owners, bounded pilots, cutover contingencies and realized benefits net of transition costs.

These concepts are already covered by the selected research procedures or can be incorporated as short task-specific checks. The review does not recommend copying entire personas to obtain them.

## Material limitations found

Several definitions combine useful concepts with fabricated professional experience or memory, mandatory rituals, arbitrary performance thresholds, unavailable systems and unrequested external actions. Embedded examples contain substantive defects: ignored multiplayer ticks and incomplete ownership checks; inconsistent pipeline state handling; precision labeled as true-positive rate; missing cohort denominators and unguarded divisions; security examples with unsafe interpolation, dropped provenance or ineffective validation; and domain templates that contradict their own stated safeguards.

The final specialized batch illustrates this pattern. The organizational psychologist encourages independent estimates but makes categorical diagnostic claims and applies uncalibrated survey thresholds. Operations contributes bottleneck analysis but gives an ambiguous dimensionally inconsistent takt-time formula. Medical billing makes unsupported universal statistical and coding claims. Real estate offers useful confirmed-versus-rumored evidence and milestone states while presuming transaction authority and broad legal rules. M&A provides migration and benefit-accounting concepts with excessive corporate milestones and implied HR authority.

Findings describe the inspected source. This is a selection and prompt/code review, not a security certification, medical/legal validation, runtime test or guarantee that every defect was found. Domain-specific claims were flagged for verification rather than independently certified. Per-definition rationale, risks, line counts and revision are retained in `agency.csv`; delegated completion JSON files retain reviewer notes.
