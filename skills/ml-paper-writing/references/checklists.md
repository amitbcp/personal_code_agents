# Manuscript handoff checks

Use the relevant items for the requested deliverable. This list is an internal quality aid, not a substitute for the current venue checklist.

## Evidence

- Main claims trace to results, proofs, or verified sources; abstract and conclusion retain the same scope.
- Baselines, data splits/grouping, tuning procedure, versions, and evaluation protocol are clear.
- Test data remained outside development and selection; limitations or contamination concerns are disclosed.
- Metrics and uncertainty have definitions, sample sizes, and calculation methods; figures/tables match source artifacts.
- Negative results, failure modes, and limits that affect the conclusions remain visible.
- Reproduction pointers identify existing configs, code revisions, data/model versions, environment, and compute conditions without exposing credentials.

## Citations and presentation

- Each new citation has verified identity and supports its nearby claim.
- Bibliography keys resolve; duplicates, metadata conflicts, and inaccessible sources are addressed or explicitly unresolved.
- Terms, notation, labels, units, captions, and cross-references agree.
- The actual build completes, and the rendered output has been inspected when formatting is in scope. Record an unavailable build/render check rather than claiming success.

## Venue-specific preparation

- Exact venue/year/track/stage instructions have been checked at their official source.
- Current template, content limits, bibliography style, supplements, anonymity, disclosures, and required statements are followed where applicable.
- Asset notices and citations are preserved. Code/data availability statements describe what actually exists and is authorized for release.
- The final handoff distinguishes a working draft, a reviewed draft with gaps, and a fully checked submission artifact. Preparing a paper does not itself submit or publish it.
